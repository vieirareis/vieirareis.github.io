ENA shipping docs

Internal tool used by the operations team of the ARM Eastern North Atlantic (ENA) atmospheric observatory, Graciosa Island, Azores, to keep the customs and shipping records of the scientific equipment at the site.

The tool uploads ZIP files with shipping documents (shipping requests, pro forma invoices and shipping forms) to a Google Drive folder owned by the operator and links them from a Google Sheet. It is not offered to the public.
